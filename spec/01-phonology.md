# 01. Phonology & Orthography

Promta (*promta*) enforces a strict **12-letter alphabet** with a one-to-one letter-to-sound correspondence.

## 2. Syllable Structure: `(C)V(N)`

* Every syllable consists of:
  1. An optional single consonant onset `(C)`
  2. A mandatory vowel nucleus `V`
  3. An optional nasal coda `(N)` (`m` or `n` only)
* **No consonant clusters** are allowed within a syllable. When adapting English or Latinate technical roots, clusters are simplified or epenthesized:
  * *translate* → **tansi** (`tan-si`, dropping `r`)
  * *prime / first* → **pime** (`pi-me`, dropping `r`)
  * *protocol* → **potokola** (`po-to-ko-la`, epenthetic `o`)
  * *clarity* → **kalara** (`ka-la-ra`, epenthetic `a`)
* **Note on Standalone Function Particles:** Monosyllabic grammatical particles (such as `por`, `bat`, `kan`, `ten`) may end in a single non-nasal consonant to keep high-frequency glue words ultra-short.
